# Ablative of View-Point

You will come across the suffix **-to** in your Pāḷi study. This is a generic suffix which can be added to any noun or adjective to create the ablative sense “*from*”.[^27]

[^27]: See §133. Syntax of the Cases in the Pali Nikayas.

rukkha (tree)

rukkhato (from the tree/s)

In the *Tipiṭaka* it often conveys the meaning of “*as*” …

te dhamme **dukkhato** samanupassati.

He regards those things **as suffering**.
