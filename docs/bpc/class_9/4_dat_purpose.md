# The dative of purpose

Another construction in Pāḷi, similar to the infinitive, is the Dative of Purpose.

samaṇo gotamo arahati bhavantaṃ kūṭadantaṃ **dassanāya** upasaṅkamituṃ ([DN5.2](https://s.dhamma.gift/dn/dn5/index.html))

Ascetic Gotama ought to approach Sir Crooked Tooth **to see** (him).

The word *dassanāya* in this sentence is called a dative of purpose and has a similar meaning to the infinitive, meaning “*for the purpose of seeing*” or “*to see*”.

These are very common in the Canon.

atha kho te bhikkhū sāvatthiyaṃ **piṇḍāya** caritvā … ([MN13](https://s.dhamma.gift/mn/mn13/index.html))

Then those monks, having walked **for alms** in Sāvatthī …
