# Compound Verbs (comp vb) in DPD

In the grammar column of DPD you will often see the tag **comp vb**, for example:

| | | | |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sacchikaroti | pr, comp vb | sacchi + karoti | personally experiences |
| sacchikatvā | abs of sacchikaroti, comp vb | sacchi + katvā | having personally experienced |
| etadavoca | aor, comp vb | etad + avoca | said this (to) |

DPD defines it as: **compound verb** – when a verb is combined with another word(s) to form a single unit.

## How It Differs From the Other Compounds

The seven types of *samāsa* we have studied are nouns, adjectives or indeclinables. A compound verb is different in three ways.

1. The last member is a **verb form**, not a noun. It takes the forms of a verb (tenses, absolutive, infinitive, participles): *sacchikaroti*, *sacchikari*, *sacchikatvā*, *sacchikātuṃ*, *sacchikata*.

2. The first member often **keeps its case ending**: *kālaṃ* + akāsi, *manasi* + karoti, *tena* + upasaṅkami. In an ordinary compound it would normally appear in its stem form; the exception is the *alutta tappurisa* ([Class 19](../class_19/4_tap.md)).

3. DPD usually gives **no compound type** (kammadhāraya, tappurisa, etc.) and **no root** for a compound verb (a few exceptions exist: *bahulīkata* is also given as kammadhāraya). To find the root, look up the verb part: *karoti*, *bhavati*, *upasaṅkamati*, and so on.

A compound verb is also not the same as a verb with a prefix (*upasagga*). In *upasaṅkamati* (upa + saṃ + √kam) the prefixes are not words of their own, and DPD gives the root √kam. In *tenupasaṅkami* the word *tena* is a separate word that has joined the verb, so it is a comp vb.

## Common Patterns

### Indeclinable + karoti / bhavati

The most frequent type. A word that is not declined joins *karoti* (does, makes) or *bhavati* (becomes).

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sacchikaroti | sacchi + karoti | personally experiences; realizes |
| pātukaroti | pātu + karoti | displays; manifests |
| pātubhavati | pātu + bhavati | appears; becomes evident |
| tuṇhībhavati | tuṇhī + bhavati | keeps silence |
| sakkaroti | sat + karoti | honours; respects |
| garukaroti | garu + karoti | honours; esteems |

With some adjectives the last vowel becomes **-ī** before *karoti*:

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bahulīkaroti | bahula > bahulī + karoti | practises often; cultivates |

### A declined word + verb

The first member keeps its case ending, and the two words are felt as one expression.

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| manasikaroti | manasi + karoti | pays attention (lit. makes in the mind) |
| kālamakāsi | kālaṃ + akāsi | died (lit. made (his) time) |
| hīnāyāvattati | hīnāya + āvattati | reverts to lay life |
| attānukkaṃseti | attānaṃ + ukkaṃseti | exalts oneself |
| dhammamaññāya | dhammaṃ + aññāya | understanding the Dhamma |

### Pronoun + verb in stock phrases

Some very frequent sutta phrases are joined by sandhi and listed in DPD as one word.

| | | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| etadavoca | etad + avoca | said this (to) |
| tenupasaṅkami | tena + upasaṅkami | approached there |
| tadavasari | tad + avasari | went there; arrived there |

## Examples

“evaṃ, devā”ti kho so puriso rañño pasenadissa kosalassa paṭissutvā yena bhagavā **tenupasaṅkami**; upasaṅkamitvā bhagavantaṃ abhivādetvā ekamantaṃ nisīdi. ekamantaṃ nisinno kho so puriso bhagavantaṃ **etadavoca** ([MN90](https://s.dhamma.gift/mn/mn90/index.html))

tenupasaṅkami < tena + upasaṅkami (aor, comp vb)

etadavoca < etad + avoca (aor, comp vb)

“Yes, sire,” the man replied to King Pasenadi of Kosala, and **went to** where the Blessed One was. Having gone there, he paid respects to the Blessed One and sat to one side. Seated to one side, the man **said this** to the Blessed One.

atha kho anāthapiṇḍiko gahapati, acirapakkante āyasmante ca sāriputte āyasmante ca ānande, **kālamakāsi** tusitaṃ kāyaṃ upapajji ([MN143](https://s.dhamma.gift/mn/mn143/index.html))

kālamakāsi < kālaṃ + akāsi (aor of kālaṃ karoti, comp vb)

Then, not long after Venerable Sāriputta and Venerable Ānanda had left, the householder Anāthapiṇḍika **died** and was reborn in the Tusita host.

cattārome, bhikkhave, iddhipādā bhāvitā **bahulīkatā** apārā pāraṃ gamanāya saṃvattanti ([SN51.1](https://s.dhamma.gift/sn/sn51/index.html#sn51.1))

bahulīkata < bahula > bahulī + kata (pp of bahulīkaroti, comp vb)

Monks, these four bases of power, when developed and **practised often**, lead from the near shore to the far shore.

tena hi, sunakkhatta, suṇāhi, sādhukaṃ **manasi karohi**; bhāsissāmī ([MN105](https://s.dhamma.gift/mn/mn105/index.html))

Then, Sunakkhatta, listen, and **pay** close **attention**; I will speak.

Note that editions differ in spacing. Here the text prints *manasi karohi* as two words, but DPD lists the unit under *manasikaroti* (comp vb). If you cannot find a word in DPD, try looking it up joined to the word next to it, or the other way round.

## How to Use the Tag

When you meet **comp vb** in DPD:

1. Read the **construction** column to see the two parts.
2. Translate the first part (an adverb, a case form or a pronoun) and the verb part separately.
3. Then translate the whole as one idea, as DPD's meaning does: *kālaṃ akāsi* “made (his) time” = “died”.
4. For the root, tense and conjugation, look up the verb part on its own.
